How to play
MouseLeft click or drag to select.
Double click selects all of one type.
Right click to move, gather, build, attack or set a rally point.
Wheel to zoom. Screen edge or arrow keys to scroll.
KeysQ W E R T, A S D F G, Z X C V B for the command card.
H for the Rajwada, M for Maharaj, full stop for idle Rayat.
Ctrl or Alt with 1 to 9 sets a group, 1 to 9 recalls it.
Space centres, Esc cancels, P pauses.
BasicsRayat gather Dhanya, Lakud, Hon and Dagad.
Build Ghar for population and a Talim for Mavale.
Advance the age at the Rajwada to unlock more.
Destroy the enemy Qila and defeat its commander.

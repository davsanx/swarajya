SWARAJYA

A real-time strategy of the Maratha rise · 1646 – 1674
“हे राज्य व्हावे हे श्रींची इच्छा”
How to play
Mouse
Left-click / drag — select
Double-click — select all of a type
Right-click — move, gather, build, attack, set rally
Wheel — zoom · Screen edge / arrows — scroll
Keys
Q W E R T · A S D F G · Z X C V B — command card
H — Rajwada · M — Maharaj · . — idle Rayat
Ctrl/Alt + 1–9 — set group · 1–9 — recall
Space — centre · Esc — cancel · P — pause
Basics
Rayat gather Dhanya, Lakud, Hon and Dagad.
Build Ghar for population, a Talim for Mavale.
Advance the age at the Rajwada to unlock more.
Destroy the enemy Qila and defeat its commander.
